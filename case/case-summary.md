## Incident Description

An employee reported receiving a suspicious email containing a link requesting the user to verify their account.

The Security Operations Center (SOC) will investigate the email to determine whether it is a phishing attempt and identify the scope of the campaign.

## Investigation Objectives

- Analyze the complete email headers
- Identify the true sending infrastructure
- Examine SPF, DKIM and DMARC results
- Extract and investigate URLs
- Analyze URLs and domains using VirusTotal
- Identify other users who received the email
- Identify users who clicked the suspicious link
- Determine the scope of the incident
- Recommend containment and remediation actions
